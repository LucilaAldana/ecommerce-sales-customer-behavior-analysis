### ecommerce-sales-customer-behavior-analysis

# Sales Performance & Customer Behavior Analysis

## by Lucila Aldana Quiñonez | Marketing Data Analyst

📄 Report available in: English - Spanish

This project analyzes four years (2015–2018) of transactional sales data from a U.S.-based e-commerce retail company to uncover insights into sales performance, customer behavior, product dynamics, and shipping patterns, with the goal of supporting data-driven decision-making across marketing, operations, and inventory management.

Key Insights:

• Sustained Growth: Sales increased ~50%, driven by a ~75% rise in order volume (not order value)

• Seasonality: Sales peak in September–December and drop in January, showing strong cyclical demand

• Revenue Concentration: A small share of high-value orders drives a large portion of revenue

• Geographic Dependency: California and New York generate over one-third of total sales

• Product Performance: Balanced categories, but revenue is concentrated in key sub-categories

• Low Product Frequency: Many products show minimal purchases → potential inventory inefficiencies

• Customer Behavior: Stable segmentation (Consumer, Corporate, Home Office) with moderate repeat purchasing

• Shipping Patterns: Standard Class (~60%) dominates, indicating low demand for premium shipping

Recommendations:

• Leverage seasonality for targeted marketing campaigns

• Optimize product assortment and reduce excess inventory

• Increase repeat purchases through loyalty strategies

• Investigate underlying drivers of growth

• Improve shipping and operational efficiency

Data Limitations:

• No quantity, cost, or discount data → no pricing/profitability analysis

• No delivery date → shipping efficiency not measurable

• No dedicated customer dataset → limited customer insights

• This project simulates a real-world business scenario where historical data is used to understand performance drivers and improve profitability through data-driven insights.

Tools Used: Python / Generative AI / Data Cleaning / EDA / KPI Analysis
